# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the core project management framework used by OctoAcme to plan, deliver, and improve cross-functional work. The approach emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, and measurable outcomes. Projects move through a defined lifecycle—initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective—so teams can align quickly, track progress consistently, and make decisions with shared context.

## OctoAcme project management approach

OctoAcme uses a lightweight but structured process designed to help teams deliver value reliably without creating unnecessary overhead. The organization starts by validating the business need and defining measurable success, then turns approved initiatives into an actionable backlog and release plan. During execution, delivery teams use a regular cadence of standups, reviews, and progress tracking to surface blockers early and maintain momentum. Quality is treated as a continuous responsibility through test automation, CI checks, security scanning, and verification before release. After each milestone or release, the team captures learnings and turns them into improvement actions.

## Summary of core workflows

OctoAcme's process is built around a repeatable cadence: confirm the need, align stakeholders, prioritize the backlog, execute in small increments, monitor quality and risks, and finish with release verification and retrospectives. Communication is kept regular and transparent through standups, weekly syncs, milestone reviews, and escalation paths when blockers or business-impacting issues arise. Team roles are deliberately defined so that product, delivery, and stakeholder responsibilities are visible and aligned. This combination of planning discipline, clear ownership, and iterative improvement helps the team deliver value while reducing surprises and encouraging learning.
